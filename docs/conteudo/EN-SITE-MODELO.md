> Capturado de `mukogawa.com` em 2026-08-01. As rotas são client-side e o idioma

## Host / Guest — não é erro

O inglês chama a facção rebelde de **the Guest**, em oposição a **the Host**. É
uso consistente no texto corrido ("the eternal conflict between the Host and the
Guest", "where the Guest cannot see clearly", "The King of the Guest"), não um
deslize nos rótulos. O português usa "Rebeldes" para a mesma facção. É o mesmo
padrão de Protogenesis/Genesis e Desvelar/Unveiled: nome diferente por idioma.

O japonês do site antigo traduz isso como 客人側 / 客人の王 ("lado do convidado",
"rei dos convidados"), acompanhando o inglês.

## História — texto integral EN

### Abertura

> Before history, a civilization went to war over the shape of the world.
>
> Much like the Norse sagas and the Greek epics, Mukō-Gawa is a deep dive into
> Christian mythology. The narrative tells of Creation and Redemption, as a
> celestial war culminates in eternal peace.
>
> Mukō-Gawa follows a civil war among a proto-human people who contend on an
> interdimensional plane — the fold between what is and what was promised. Two
> orders emerge from one bloodline: those who would hold the plane closed, and
> those who would tear it open.

### Three series

**01 Saga** — FIVE SEASONS // FIVE BOOKS // MAIN MANGA ARC

> Saga is the tale of redemption told from the other side. A brutal war fought
> through sword and mind, where legions descend upon their foes and generals
> wield not only power, but strategy. Divided into five seasons, Saga unfolds the
> eternal conflict between the Host and the Guest, as the fate of mankind hangs
> in the balance.

**02 Genesis** — PREQUEL // FEATURE FILM & BOOK

> Genesis is the origin: a mythic retelling of what transpired before the war
> began, standing to Saga as The Silmarillion stands to The Lord of the Rings.
> Planned as a feature film, Genesis will be released between Seasons Two and
> Three, offering depth, context, and ancient history.

**03 Unveiled** — EPILOGUE // FEATURE FILM & BOOK

> Unveiled is the final light. After the climactic end of Season Five, many will
> be left wondering what became of their favourite characters. Unveiled is a
> post-series film, created to bring resolution, mystery and closure — the final
> act in the Mukō-Gawa journey.

### The Defiled Well

> After the Rebellion, the Defilers discovered that the Living Waters of the High
> Heavens no longer welcomed them. What once healed now burned. In defiance, they
> stole the sacred waters and brought them into the Abyss. There, under the gaze
> of King Snake, the stolen grace was twisted through forbidden alchemy, giving
> rise to the Defiled Well.
>
> The Well offers restoration, but at a price. Each visit corrodes the soul.
> Forms twist, essence decays, and some are defiled beyond recognition, becoming
> Tormentors — wretches unleashed upon mankind to sow confusion and dread.
>
> Though the Well grants dark power, there is still reverence for those who
> retain their original form. To remain uncorrupted is rare among the fallen, and
> it speaks of a terrifying strength. For in the Abyss, corruption is power, but
> endurance is glory.

### War for Mankind

> During the Age of Stars, the Heavenly Council was assembled, and the Almighty
> revealed His design: to bring forth a new world, an inhabitable planet where a
> new species would rise. They would be called mankind. Unlike the Eternals, they
> would not be born in hosts but would multiply across the earth, shaped from
> dust yet bearing the image of the One who formed them. In time, they would be
> welcomed into the fellowship of Heaven.
>
> But then came the greater mystery, one that stirred the heavens and set
> eternity on edge: the Eternals would serve mankind in this age. And in the age
> to come, mankind would serve as arbiters over them.
>
> It was in that moment that Helel's heart turned to stone. He refused the
> decree. What began as pride became open defiance, and in his heart he coveted
> the Throne itself. No longer veiled, his rebellion erupted against the Most
> High, igniting the civil war among the Eternals. In his eyes, the subjugation
> of mankind is not merely strategy, but self-preservation. In secret, he builds
> a paradise — a godless utopia, where the self is enthroned and the Architect
> forgotten.
>
> And though mortals remain, for the most part, blind to the Eternal War, it is
> no less real. It surrounds them, shaping kingdoms, stirring hearts, shifting
> the course of history.

Fala do Helel: *"To serve these lesser creatures? To be judged by them? Absurd."*

História da Redenção"). Conferido no build. Nada a reintroduzir — só traduzir

> Mukō-Gawa is a work rooted in Christian mythology. While remaining faithful to
> the biblical narrative, it draws upon the discipline of biblical synergism — an
> approach that honours Scripture while engaging with history, tradition and
> theological imagination to build a world that fits within the contours of what
> the Bible reveals. Though the writer holds biblical accuracy in high regard,
> Mukō-Gawa is ultimately a work of fiction. The events of Scripture remain
> untouched, but what happens between the lines is shaped by fantasy. Angels
> clash behind the veil. Powers move unseen. Choices ripple across realms.
>
> It must also be said: there are many schools of biblical interpretation. To
> choose one path is, inevitably, to leave others behind. Mukō-Gawa does not
> pretend to please every doctrine; it seeks, rather, to tell the greatest story
> of all — the Tale of Redemption.

## Personagens — verbetes integrais EN

Abertura: *"The cast is vast. These pages introduce the nine classes, one at a time."*

### CLASS 01 — GABRIEL — HERALD // COHORT

> Cohorts are the foundational workforce of the Host. Formed with willing hearts
> and bound by duty, they carry out the will of the High Heavens with unwavering
> resolve. In the Age of Stars, those Eternals who gravitated toward service were
> appointed to this rank. Bearing a single pair of wings, Cohorts are the
> backbone of the Host — soldiers, messengers, and whatever is needed in between.
> They may not wield the might of an Archon or the mystery of a Principality, but
> their loyalty shapes the outcome of every war.
>
> Among them, Gabriel is renowned. A herald of the Most High, he is rarely seen
> with a weapon. Though a formidable warrior, Gabriel avoids combat — not out of
> fear for the battle, but fear of what his hands might do. Instead, he walks
> into the fray unarmed, bearing not steel, but the weight of the Word. His voice
> has silenced wars and stirred kings to repentance. In him, the calling of a
> Cohort is fully seen — not to dominate, but to serve with power, purpose, and
> peace.

### CLASS 01 — AMUNET — DEFILED // COHORT

> Amunet is one of the series' most visually distinctive characters, and his
> appearance is a direct illustration of what the Defiled Well does over time.
> His skin is dark, scaled hide. His features are thick and heavy, suggesting the
> ancient crocodiles of the Nile delta.
>
> His most defining feature: his right arm — or what was once his right arm — has
> become a grotesque cluster of fused limbs, each with its own hands and fingers,
> intertwined and writhing like a knot of serpents. The individual hands flex
> involuntarily. The mass has a life of its own.

Nota: aqui está, no original, o mesmo desvio de ponto de vista que o VOZ.md
aponta no português ("é um dos personagens visualmente mais marcantes da série").
O EN diz "the series' most visually distinctive characters". Ou seja, **não é
defeito da nossa versão PT: veio assim.** A decisão continua sendo do Bruno.

### CLASS 02 — MICHAEL — THE WARMONGER // ARCHON

> Archons are the mightiest warriors among the Host. In the Age Before, they were
> the Eternals drawn to strength, and strength was given to them in abundance. No
> other rank matches their raw mettle or resilience, making them decisive figures
> in times of war. They are the only echelon entrusted with Rift-Tearing weapons
> — masterworks of Zorphael, the forgemaster of the Host — each forged in flame
> and consecrated by the Architect's own hand. These weapons can rend the fabric
> of space itself, opening portals across realms, battlefields, and even the
> Veil. Archons bear two pairs of wings: one broad and commanding, the other
> smaller and swift — crafted for violent thrusts and perfect aerial
> maneuvering.
>
> Among them, none stands higher than Michael, the Warmonger. Unyielding.
> Undefeated. His presence alone strikes fear into the hearts of Heaven's
> enemies. His spear, Veritas, was the first weapon drawn from Zorphael's forge
> when the war began. No hand but Michael's has ever wielded it.

### CLASS 02 — SATIS — FALLEN BLADE // ARCHON

> Satis is striking: an Eternal of measured elegance whose poise survives even
> the progressive damage of the Well. Her hair is white and gleaming, with a
> quiet luster that seems at odds with the environment she inhabits. Dark armour
> adorned with faded sigils encases her form — the engravings nearly worn away by
> time and battle. She carries the Onyx Shard across her back, its shattered tip
> somehow making it more dangerous rather than less.
>
> Eldritch markings twist along her arms and legs, their form stretched just
> beyond natural proportions — the Well's corruption spreading slowly across her.

### CLASS 03 — CELESTE — WEAVER // PRINCIPALITY

> Principalities are the princes of knowledge, those among the Eternals who, in
> the Age Before, gravitated toward wisdom. Long before the civil war, they were
> architects of literature, stewards of heavenly order, and weavers of celestial
> power. Of all the Host, none surpass them in the mastery of arcane forces.
> Their magic is elegant and exacting, often written in radiant script that coils
> through the air as it is spoken — each word a key, each symbol a gate.
>
> Celeste is a living testament to what it means to be a Principality. Ever
> present in councils of war, her voice carries weight among commanders and sages
> alike. Yet when battle begins, she does not stand behind. She lifts her hand,
> and the skies answer, unleashing wonders that can reshape the battlefield for
> her brethren.

### CLASS 04 — ARIEL — VIALBEARER // VIRTUE

> Virtues are formidable beings whose power flows not from arcane knowledge, like
> the Principalities, but from innate emanation. Their presence shapes not only
> the battlefield, but entire regions — bringing strength, restoration, and
> clarity where there was once chaos. They move between realms, often unseen,
> serving as vessels through which healing and deliverance are poured out upon
> mankind. Marked by a regal pair of horns that crown their heads — a symbol of
> Second Echelon authority — Virtues bear a single pair of wings. Though seldom
> seen in open combat, their influence is undeniable. Before the war, they were
> the Eternals who longed for beauty: artists, dreamers, and singers of light,
> who once filled the cosmos with wonder.
>
> Among them, Ariel is a beacon of hope. Wherever she walks, healing follows. She
> carries sacred vials of the living waters of the High Heavens, a single drop
> enough to raise a fallen warrior. Ariel is patient and measured, yet never
> distant — her bond with her brother Tesserion unbreakable.

### CLASS 05 — TESSERION — STORMBEARER // POWER

> Powers, also called Custodians, are unlike their Ancient and Eternal siblings.
> While the Ancients bear the mark of the Elders, and the Eternals were summoned
> in a single breath, the Powers came later — born during the great flourishing
> of the Age of Stars. As the cosmos expanded and the architecture of creation
> grew vast, the Eternals cried out for aid. In response, the Custodians were
> forged: colossal beings shaped by the will of the Most High, with the hands of
> their elder siblings guiding the work. Monumental in stature, like titans of
> light and steel, they became the wardens of the Far Heavens — ensuring
> structure, harmony and balance. Though youngest among the Host, their presence
> is unmatched. Each Custodian is singular in form; some winged, others not.
>
> Among them stands Tesserion, the Stormbearer. Towering like a mountain clad in
> armour, he wields a blade the size of a citadel. His lion-helm has become a
> symbol of sanctuary. Though few words pass his lips, he shares an
> indestructible bond with his sister, Ariel.

### CLASS 06 — KHARZUL — SCOUT // DOMINAR — OF THE GUEST

> Dominars stand at the head of the Second Echelon, marked by two sets of wings
> and regal horns that crown their heads. These are the Eternals who, in the Age
> Before, gravitated toward both strength and knowledge, and in that union became
> something distinct. Though not as strong as an Archon, nor as arcane as a
> Principality, the Dominars excel in both. That balance — of body and mind,
> blade and spell — makes them dangerous in ways few can anticipate. Strategists
> and commanding presences, they often serve as the bridge between the Hosts
> above and below, leading charges where angels fear to tread.
>
> Among them once stood Kharzul. Seduced by ambition, he aligned with the
> Defilers, abandoning the Light for the promise of ascent. His most recent
> defeat came at the hands of Michael — not only defeat, but humiliation. His
> horns are jagged, his skin darkened, his name disgraced. Stripped of rank and
> discarded by the Prince of Babylon, he was reassigned as a mere scout. And yet
> Kharzul endures: cunning, patient, watchful. A tactician still. A shadow not
> yet silenced.

### CLASS 07 — ORTHRAX — THE FALLEN EYE // OPHANIM — OF THE GUEST

> The Ophanim belong to the First Echelon: beings of terrible and radiant beauty,
> formed of interlocking wheels forged from the oldest metals of creation. Their
> frames burn with eyes that do not blink or rest. While the Seraphim fix their
> gaze upon the Almighty, the Ophanim cast their vision outward, to the farthest
> reaches of the cosmos. They do not speak as others speak, but resonate in
> Ophanix — a language not vocalized but released. With each turn of their
> wheels, waves of compressed truth ripple through the realms. Their speech is
> vibration; their song, revelation. Few can decipher it. Fewer still can answer.
>
> And yet, one among them fell. Orthrax was once a pillar of unity, a vigilant
> eye that bound the heavens. Now he serves the King Snake, sent into regions
> where the Guest cannot see clearly. His presence grants the Defiled sight — but
> at a cost. For when an Ophanim enters the field, the heavens must answer. The
> stakes rise. The veil burns. Orthrax is no longer merely a watcher. He is a
> warning.

### CLASS 08 — HELEL — KING OF THE GUEST // CHERUB

> The Cherubim are beings of awe and wonder, immense in beauty and mystery.
> Formed in the first breath of the Almighty's will, they predate the Eternals
> and surpass them in power. Their true forms are otherworldly: wings upon wings,
> bodies of flame and wind, faces that shift like fire. Yet among the Host they
> often appear as great beasts — majestic, soaring like omens through the
> expanse. Desiring fellowship with their younger kin, they took on Visages:
> humanoid forms chosen not by need, but affection.
>
> Among them stood Helel, radiant and mighty, a prince robed in splendour,
> trusted with mysteries. But in time the light turned inward. He questioned why
> glory returned to the Throne, why power was shared, why dust should bear the
> image of the Almighty. He became the first to lie, to turn, to spark rebellion.
> A third of the Eternals followed him. For this he was cast down, his wings
> severed. Once the dragon of old, he now slithers in shadow. Yet his beauty
> endures, untarnished by the Defiled Well. He is the architect of war. The King
> of the Guest.

### CLASS 09 — SERAPHIM — THE BURNING ONES // FIRST ECHELON

> The Seraphim are the first of the Ancient, eldest among their kind and heads of
> the First Echelon. Of all the Host, they alone were formed with the gift to
> comprehend — not merely to know, but to understand, deeply and in full. Their
> minds move with the breath of the Architect Himself. For this reason, they have
> never left the Throne Room. Not bound by command or chain, they remain by
> choice — captivated. For once they beheld the glory of the One upon the Throne,
> they could not look away.
>
> Their worship is endless, not because they were told to sing, but because they
> cannot stop seeing. Their voices are deep and layered, echoing through the High
> Heavens like the roar of flame. They bear six wings, countless eyes, and a fire
> none but they can endure. Wherever they go, they burn. Their presence is
> overwhelming, their insight unfathomable. These are the Burning Ones — the
> first to see, and the last to turn away.

## Autor e artistas — EN

**ISRAEL SUBIRA** — WRITER // CREATOR OF MUKŌ-GAWA

> Israel Subira is the writer and creator of Mukō-Gawa, working from a background
> in theology and fantasy. He began the project roughly seven years ago, and it
> has grown since into a manga, a completed novel cycle, and the groundwork for
> an animated adaptation.
>
> It has since grown into a series of books with a substantial social media
> following and no small amount of expectation behind it. The manga has been in
> development with Guilherme Match, a widely respected Brazilian illustrator.
>
> His time as a dungeon master in tabletop role-playing games shaped the project
> deeply — his own homebrew campaigns were the training ground before he entered
> fantasy as a writer. Israel's method is a hybrid of outline and exploratory
> writing: he plans the most critical narrative points ahead, then leaves room for
> a strong cast to run with the story and grow alongside the main arc.
>
> He is passionate about art and literature, and has several other unpublished
> works in the works.

**DANIEL BRETAS** — CONCEPT ART

> Bretas drew the first round of original concept art for Mukō-Gawa — the
> character studies and class designs that set the visual language of the series.
> He won the Brasil Manga Awards in 2015, and in 2023 took two prizes at the 35th
> Troféu HQ Mix: best production in other media for Calaboca e Escuta, and best
> graphic design for Colorbar.

**MATCH** — MANGA

> Guilherme Match has a long history in illustration, much of it spent in
> advertising, but comics are where he found his true passion. His work has been
> published by JBC, including Kophee and his latest comic, Soundtrack.
